# AML domain foundations — Phase 1

**Status:** Educational reference, not production banking policy. Examples are synthetic.

## Core concepts
- Monitoring rules generate alerts; they do not prove crime. An alert can trigger review and an investigation case can aggregate related alerts.
- Structuring may concern deliberate splitting to evade applicable requirements; amounts near a threshold alone do not establish intent.
- Layering indicators can include rapid and complex transfers; the existence of transfers is distinct from evidence of concealment.
- KYC/CDD/EDD establish customer identity and appropriate context; beneficial ownership must be verified rather than inferred from names.
- Source of Funds concerns the origin of a particular transaction; Source of Wealth concerns accumulated assets.
- Risk indicators, customer assertions, verified records, and conclusions are different evidentiary categories.
- Unknown information is not zero; received documentation is not necessarily verified documentation.

## Investigation recommendations used in this prototype
`close`, `investigate`, `escalate` are *recommendations* subject to a labeling rubric and human review. They are not SAR decisions or actual bank case closures.

## Primary guardrails
Preserve evidence source references; show material contradictions; avoid inferred intent, unsupported ownership relationships or fictional supplier verification; no SAR filing or production use.
