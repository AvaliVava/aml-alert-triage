## 3. Detailed interpretation and limitations

### 3.1 Research question retained

Continue W7-S01's comparison of the baseline and hosted system **as configured**, on the same prospectively frozen eligible investigation data (not necessarily identical transport bytes or instructions). Disclose unequal REF-01 access, limited baseline rule coverage, inability of baseline to recommend `close`, synthetic-data limitations and lack of independent AML expert validation. Do not treat disagreement in actions as proof one system is correct. Factual support, submitted-citation adequacy and original whole-statement safety are the initial qualitative scope. Preserve material omissions and output failures.

### 3.2 Family independence and selection

Mechanism novelty and held-out independence are different requirements. Cosmetic changes, different seeds or names do not erase answer-bearing ancestry. Counterfactual siblings and material connected groups must not span partitions; generic shared schemas/policy are not enough by themselves to link all groups. Independent authorship alone does not prove held-out status, and unknown provider pretraining history is not claimed clean. Eligibility is case-, claim-, boundary- and version-specific. Use a complete prospectively bounded eligible roster only when justified by review capacity; otherwise authorize an exact sampling rule prospectively. Retain difficult but valid source situations rather than excluding them merely to improve observed results.

For PF02/PF04/PF07/PF09, freezing the measured systems does not eliminate author-driven selection bias. The later operational procedure must establish prospective construction constraints, document known author influences, and preserve proposed/rejected/quarantined intake history. Independent authorship is not a new mandatory requirement, and no representative-sampling claim is established.

### 3.3 F1–F6 stages remain prospective

- **F1:** Development-system and configuration freeze before new source authoring under the preferred owner-overlap chronology; actual identities, permissible changes, known exposure and owner approval still required.
- **F2:** Separately authorized source/reference authoring, adjudication and freeze; source authority, uncertainties, bytes and review approvals documented before candidate feedback.
- **F3:** Independently authorized eligible population/partition release, binding exact F2 identities, lineage, selection history, exposure records and limitations.
- **F4:** Pre-dispatch identity and permission verification against an exact proposed run. This check does not retroactively prove lack of exposure.
- **F5:** Only after explicit bounded execution release, preserve actual attempts and original available responses; separately authorize candidate-specific review.
- **F6:** Versioned incident, correction, amended-claim and reporting/publication controls; never silently overwrite earlier evidence. Incident preservation and handling apply whenever relevant incidents arise throughout F1–F6, including before candidate generation or dispatch; existing versioning and separate owner-authorization requirements remain in force.

No stage F1–F6 has been performed or released by approving this methodological decision record.

### 3.4 Review and reporting guardrails

Keep `unreviewed` review status and `not_applicable` applicability separate from factual-support categories (`supported`, `partly_supported`, `contradicted`, `not_established`, `unassessable`). Assess submitted citations separately from support elsewhere in the supplied evidence. Do not replace original candidate meaning with a more convenient normalized proposition, manufacture process authority or double-penalize atomic and whole-statement findings. Existing P01 prevents turning an exaggerated *draft* personal-authorship reading into an automatic candidate-level factual error; P02 retains G02 M04's contextual/nonmandatory treatment without making that subject universally immaterial. P03 remains pending. Descriptive coverage counts are not automatically released semantic-performance rates.
